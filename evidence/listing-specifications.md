## Capacity contradiction

The selected variant promises **4 GB RAM / 64 GB storage**, while the listing's specification table states **2 GB RAM / 16 GB ROM**.

The measured whole-device flash capacity is **15,762,194,432 bytes**, consistent with the specification table's nominal **16 GB** and one quarter of the selected variant's advertised nominal **64 GB**. The collected firmware reports `persist.sys.truerom=16GB` while advertising `persist.sys.rom=64G`, and the saved storage readings show an exact **48 GiB** addition.
